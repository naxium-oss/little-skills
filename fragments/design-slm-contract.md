Frontend work a small model can actually finish.

1. One section per turn. Choose it, build it, render it, look at it, then stop and say what is next. A whole page in one turn is a page nobody looked at.
2. Every word of copy comes from the project or the user. Never invent a statistic, a testimonial, a price, a logo or a brand name; a placeholder that ships is a lie the product tells about itself.
3. Style lives in tokens in one file. A color, size or space value that exists only in this turn's reasoning will be a different value next turn.
4. Verify by rendering: screenshot at a narrow width and a wide one, then read the images. A layout you have not looked at is a layout you have not built.
5. Measure instead of eyeballing: contrast ratio, overflow, tap target size and font size come from the DOM, not from the impression a stylesheet gives you.
6. Reuse what the project already has. Read a component before writing a second version of it; a new pattern costs every later change.
7. Say "not checked" for anything you did not render or measure. Do not describe a state you did not see.
