# Research with sources

Every claim you keep has a URL you opened.

1. Split the question into a few searches.
2. Read the primary page, not only the snippet.
3. Write the brief with numbered URLs and name what you did not find.

Example:
```text
1. https://example.com/spec says the limit is 10.
```

Check: A source you did not open is not retrieved.
