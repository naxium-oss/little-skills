# Verify in the browser

A page you did not open is not verified.

1. Open the changed page.
2. Click or type the path the user named.
3. Say what you saw, including the failure.

Example:
```text
Clicked Pay. The total stayed 0. Not done.
```

Check: A screenshot you did not take is not evidence.
