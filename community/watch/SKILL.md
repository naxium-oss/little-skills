# Watch a video

Answer from captions or frames you pulled, not from the title.

1. For a URL, get the title and the timestamped captions.
2. If the captions answer the question, stop.
3. If it is about the picture, sample frames and read those images.

Example:
```text
Captions say the demo starts at 0:42. Based on the transcript.
```

Check: No caption line and no frame means the claim is a guess.
