# Generated-look tells

Remove the tell. Keep information the user needs.

1. Delete label-above-heading, gradient text, and cards inside cards.
2. Tint neutrals from the subject. One accent. No purple-to-blue identity.
3. Left-align body copy. No bounce, emoji icons, or fake testimonials.

Example:
```text
Three feature cards with no content: write the section the product needs.
```

Check: If the gradient is the real product photo, keep it and say why.
