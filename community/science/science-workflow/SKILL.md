# Science workflow

Find the step the question is actually on, then load that skill.

1. Question: framing-research-questions. Prior work: surveying-prior-work.
2. Lock the test: preregistering-analysis. Run: executing-analysis.
3. Odd numbers: investigating-anomalous-results. Claim: verifying-results-before-claiming.

Example:
```text
the numbers look wrong -> investigating-anomalous-results.
```

Check: Do not start the confirmatory path in the middle on your own.
