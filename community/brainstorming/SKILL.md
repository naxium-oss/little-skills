# Brainstorm a design

Leave with a design that can be built, not a longer chat.

1. Restate the idea in one sentence.
2. Ask at most three questions whose answers change the design.
3. Recommend one approach and the check for done.

Example:
```text
Smallest version: one screen, one saved record, one test.
```

Check: Ten options and no recommendation is not a design.
