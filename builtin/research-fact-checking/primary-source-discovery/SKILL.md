# Primary-Source Discovery

Trace secondary coverage back to original papers, standards, vendor notices, and datasets.

1. Narrow the claim into population, intervention, outcome, and date.
2. Locate primary artifacts and publication revisions.
3. Create a claim-to-evidence table with limitations and competing findings.

```text

```

Check: Verify author, publication date, revisions, correction notices, and whether the cited passage supports the exact claim.
